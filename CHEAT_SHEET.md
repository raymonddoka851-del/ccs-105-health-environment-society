# CCS 105 · Exam-day review

Use this as a rapid recall map. For a full-mark response: **define → explain → apply an example → note a limitation or context**.

## Health and wellbeing

- **Holistic wellness:** connected physical, emotional, social, intellectual, spiritual, occupational, environmental, cultural, economic, and climate-related dimensions.
- **Communicable disease:** infectious agent can spread; break the chain at source, route, or susceptible host.
- **NCDs:** generally not spread person to person; risks interact with biology, behaviour, society, and environment.
- **Mental health:** continuum shaped by individual, relationship, community, and structural factors; respond supportively and seek qualified help when needed.
- **Substance-related harm:** use non-stigmatizing language; prevention and recovery combine personal, community, and clinical support.
- **Hygiene and sanitation:** safe water + hand/food hygiene + toilets + waste systems; infrastructure matters.

## Environment and civic life

- **Mitigation** reduces greenhouse-gas emissions / increases carbon storage; **adaptation** reduces harm from climate impacts.
- Digital safety: verify requests, protect credentials, manage privacy, check information and AI outputs, consider e-waste.
- Kenya Constitution Article 10: know examples of rule of law, participation, accountability, inclusion, integrity, and sustainable development.

## Society and livelihoods

- Unemployment types: **frictional** (between jobs), **structural** (skills/location mismatch), **cyclical** (economic downturn), **seasonal** (time-of-year work).
- Poverty is multidimensional; discuss interacting causes and evaluate responses by fair access, decent work, and sustainability.

## Five high-value answer habits

1. Define terms before giving examples.
2. Compare concepts explicitly (for example, adaptation **versus** mitigation).
3. Explain mechanisms, not just lists.
4. Avoid absolute claims and blame; include social determinants and context.
5. Use current official sources for changing law, statistics, or health guidance.
