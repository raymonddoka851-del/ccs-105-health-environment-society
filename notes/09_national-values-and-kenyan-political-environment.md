# 09 · National Values and Kenyan Political Environment

## Civic foundations

National values are shared principles intended to guide public life and institutions. Kenya’s Constitution, Article 10, identifies national values and principles of governance including **patriotism, national unity, sharing and devolution of power, the rule of law, democracy and participation of the people, human dignity, equity, social justice, inclusiveness, equality, human rights, non-discrimination, protection of marginalized groups, good governance, integrity, transparency, accountability, and sustainable development**.

## Apply, do not merely list

| Value | Meaning in practice |
|---|---|
| Rule of law | Public power and citizens’ conduct are governed by law, applied fairly |
| Participation | People can take part in public decisions through lawful processes |
| Accountability and transparency | Decisions and use of public resources can be explained and scrutinized |
| Equity and inclusion | Fair access and attention to different needs and historical barriers |
| National unity | Shared civic belonging while respecting diversity and devolution |
| Integrity | Honest conduct and resistance to abuse of entrusted power |

## Political environment

The political environment includes institutions, laws, public participation, leadership, policy choices, and relationships among national and county levels of government. Devolution brings some decision-making and services closer to communities; citizens can engage through elections, public participation, lawful advocacy, and oversight.
