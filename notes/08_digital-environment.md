# 08 · Digital Environment

## What it includes

The **digital environment** is the connected space of devices, networks, platforms, applications, content, and users through which people communicate, learn, work, and access services. A digital footprint is the information created or shared through online activity; some traces are visible and others are collected in the background.

![Course-source illustration of digital technologies and the environment](../assets/figures/topic-8-digital-environment-p01-i01.jpg)

## Participate safely and thoughtfully

- **Privacy:** share only what is needed; review permissions and audience settings.
- **Security:** use unique passwords, multi-factor authentication when available, updates, and caution with links or attachments.
- **Scams and fraud:** check sender identity and requests through a separate trusted channel; do not disclose credentials or one-time codes.
- **Digital wellbeing:** set boundaries, check information before sharing, and seek help for harmful online conduct.
- **AI:** verify important outputs, protect personal information, check sources and bias, and disclose or cite use when course rules require it.
- **Environment:** devices and data services consume materials and energy; extend device life, repair where practical, and use responsible e-waste channels.

## Evaluate a digital claim

Check who published it, what evidence supports it, when it was updated, what purpose it serves, and whether independent reliable sources agree. Popularity or polished presentation is not proof.
