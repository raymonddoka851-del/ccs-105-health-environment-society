# CCS 105 · Answer guide

Use these as marking points, not scripts to memorize. For each answer, check accuracy, explanation, relevant example, and appropriate qualification.

## 01 · Holistic wellness
- **H01-Q1:** Wellness considers interconnected physical, emotional, social, intellectual, spiritual, occupational, environmental, cultural, economic, and climate-related factors, not only whether illness is present.
- **H01-Q2:** Any four dimensions with distinct, accurate examples; e.g., physical—activity/rest; social—supportive relationships; intellectual—learning; environmental—safe water.
- **H01-Q3:** Explain a pathway: unsafe housing can increase exposure to hazards; low income can limit access to nutritious food or care. Avoid implying individual choice alone explains outcomes.

## 02 · Communicable diseases
- **H02-Q1:** Agent, reservoir/source, exit, transmission, entry, susceptible host. Interrupt with vaccination, sanitation, ventilation, hand hygiene, vector control, or other relevant measure.
- **H02-Q2:** Water-borne spread can be reduced with safe water, sanitation, and food hygiene; vector-borne spread with nets, repellents, and vector control.
- **H02-Q3:** Many illnesses share symptoms; diagnosis may require history, examination, or tests. Antibiotics do not treat viruses; qualified clinicians guide care.

## 03 · Noncommunicable diseases
- **H03-Q1:** Conditions generally not transmitted person to person; examples include cardiovascular disease, cancer, chronic respiratory disease, and diabetes.
- **H03-Q2:** Modifiable examples: tobacco exposure, inactivity; non-modifiable: age, inherited susceptibility. These alter risk; they do not guarantee an outcome.
- **H03-Q3:** Screening identifies possible risk/early signs and may require follow-up; diagnosis is a clinical conclusion. Access, income, environment, and services shape prevention.

## 04 · Mental health
- **H04-Q1:** Wellbeing varies over time and circumstance; a person can experience distress without a diagnosed disorder, and support needs vary.
- **H04-Q2:** Any three levels, such as physical health, coping and life events, relationships, community belonging, discrimination, income, or service access.
- **H04-Q3:** Listen without judgment, ask what help is wanted, encourage qualified support if distress persists or impairs life; urgent safety risk calls for immediate local emergency/clinical help.

## 05 · Substance use
- **H05-Q1:** These terms describe different patterns and levels of harm; disorder requires qualified assessment, and stigmatizing labels are not diagnoses.
- **H05-Q2:** Interacting influences can include availability, stress/trauma, peer setting, health, economic conditions, and service access.
- **H05-Q3:** Combine accurate education, supportive community/family, harm prevention, and accessible clinical care. Suspected overdose or immediate danger requires urgent medical help.

## 06 · Hygiene and sanitation
- **H06-Q1:** Hygiene is health-protective practice; sanitation is safe management of waste and environmental conditions.
- **H06-Q2:** Examples: safe water/storage, handwashing, safe food handling, toilets, drainage, and waste disposal.
- **H06-Q3:** People cannot reliably practise safe routines without enabling infrastructure; community systems prevent contamination beyond one household.

## 07 · Climate change
- **H07-Q1:** Mitigation addresses emissions/carbon storage; adaptation prepares for or reduces harm from climate effects. Examples must show the relevant mechanism.
- **H07-Q2:** Heat can raise health risks and reduce outdoor work; drought/flood can disrupt water, food, housing, or income.
- **H07-Q3:** Exposure and recovery capacity differ by location, income, health, housing, and services; plans should include affected communities and distribute protection fairly.

## 08 · Digital environment
- **H08-Q1:** Connected devices, networks, applications, content, and users; footprint is information generated or shared through online activity.
- **H08-Q2:** Verify identity via another channel; inspect the request and destination; never share passwords or one-time codes; use official contact details.
- **H08-Q3:** AI may fabricate facts or citations, reflect bias, or mishandle private data. Verify against credible sources and follow course disclosure rules.

## 09 · National values and civic life
- **H09-Q1:** Any four Article 10 values with explanation (e.g., rule of law means public authority acts under law; participation gives people a role in decisions).
- **H09-Q2:** Participation surfaces public needs and scrutiny; transparency/accountability allow officials to explain decisions and respond to concerns.
- **H09-Q3:** Unity is shared civic belonging and cooperation; uniformity would erase differences. Unity can respect cultural diversity and devolution.

## 10 · Unemployment and poverty
- **H10-Q1:** Frictional—transition/search; structural—skills/location mismatch; cyclical—economic downturn; seasonal—work varies by season.
- **H10-Q2:** Job loss lowers income and access to essentials; poverty can restrict education, transport, or tools needed to find work. These are possible pathways, not inevitabilities.
- **H10-Q3:** Name a strategy, explain how it can create or enable decent work, consider who can access it, identify risks/limits, and assess long-term viability.
