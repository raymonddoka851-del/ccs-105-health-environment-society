# Question bank

Use these prompts after studying a topic. Write your answer before searching the
notes; then record what you missed in the progress tracker.

- [ ] Define the key terms introduced in each topic.
- [ ] Explain the central idea of each topic in your own words.
- [ ] Compare two related terms, processes, or methods from the course.
- [ ] Reproduce one worked example without referring to the source.
- [ ] Apply one idea from the course to a new example or context.
- [ ] Identify one misconception or error that could lose marks.
- [ ] Revisit every question you could not answer confidently.
