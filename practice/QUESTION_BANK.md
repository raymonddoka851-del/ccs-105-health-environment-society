# CCS 105 · Topic-linked question bank

Attempt closed-book. Include definitions, reasoning, and examples; use the [answer key](ANSWER_KEY.md) to check, then record results in the [progress tracker](../PROGRESS_TRACKER.md).

## 01 · Holistic wellness
### H01-Q1
Define holistic wellness and explain why it is broader than absence of disease.
### H01-Q2
Name four dimensions of wellness and give one example of each.
### H01-Q3
Explain one way social or environmental conditions can affect individual wellbeing.

## 02 · Communicable diseases
### H02-Q1
Outline the chain of infection and identify two links that prevention can interrupt.
### H02-Q2
Compare transmission through contaminated water with vector-borne transmission; give one prevention measure for each.
### H02-Q3
Explain why symptoms alone cannot reliably establish whether an infection is bacterial or viral.

## 03 · Noncommunicable diseases
### H03-Q1
Define an NCD and name three major groups.
### H03-Q2
Distinguish modifiable from non-modifiable risk factors, with two examples of each.
### H03-Q3
Explain why screening is not the same as diagnosis and why social conditions matter in prevention.

## 04 · Mental health
### H04-Q1

### H04-Q2
Describe three interacting influences on mental wellbeing.
### H04-Q3

## 05 · Substance use

### H05-Q2

### H05-Q3

## 06 · Hygiene and sanitation

### H06-Q2

### H06-Q3
