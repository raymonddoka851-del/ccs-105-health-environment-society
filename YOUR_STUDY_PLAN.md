# Your study plan

## Weekly study cycle

- [ ] Preview the course outline and choose this week's topics.
- [ ] Study one or two notes per session.
- [ ] Do a closed-book recall after each note.
- [ ] Revisit weak topics within 48 hours.
- [ ] Complete a mixed-topic review at the end of the week.

## Exam-week cycle

- [ ] Review the progress tracker and prioritize low-confidence topics.
- [ ] Rework examples without looking at their solutions.
- [ ] Search the notes for definitions and distinctions you cannot recall.
- [ ] Complete the question bank and record errors in the tracker.
- [ ] Protect time for sleep and a final light review.
